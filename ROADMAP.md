# WesFinance-Hub Roadmap

Campus finance and student club budget tracking roadmap.

## v1.1 — Allocation & Expense Tracking

- **Budget Dashboard**: Visual ledger for club allocations, reimbursements, and expense categorizations.
- **CSV & Ledger Importer**: Direct parser for student government budget manifests.

## v1.2 — Audit & Forecast Reports

- **Funding Allocation Models**: Historical trend analysis for semester budget requests.
- **Export Pipeline**: Accessible PDF and spreadsheet summaries.

## Out of Scope

- Direct bank account credentials storage
- Commercial payment processing
